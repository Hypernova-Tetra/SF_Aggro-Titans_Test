Starting out as an ambitious mod for only Supreme to restore and overhaul the supplied content we were given throughout the endgame's final moments.

After several members of the modding community went under the hood and discovered plentiful unused things that Supreme was unfortunately never given the chance to show off, this mod aims to give full functionality of what was left behind, along with some creative freedom to shake up the fight so that Supreme's animations aren't a complete 1-to-1 with Giganto.

As of version 1.20 and onward of this mod, "Aggro Titans+" now includes all 4 of the Titans. Updates to Supreme have been made to further overhaul his fight to make it the best it can be while remaining as stable as possible. There's been a lot of adjustments across all the Titans I don't feel like explaining them. To keep a bullet list of what I've done with the mod.



##### **Giganto (Giant01)**

* Super Transformation cutscene is different across Base Story, Battle Rush, and Master King's Trial.
* "Super Jump" attack will play more.
* Restored unused "Howl" attack and added sound.
* Restored unused alternate Cyloop Counter animation for Phase 2.
* Overhauled his "immediateActions" parameters and some attack params.
* Phase 2 can now be skippable.
* Updated Giganto "Counter\_Bite\_Start" Animation to use an unused bite attack from other animation files.
* Updated Giganto "Counter\_Bite\_End" Camera to be an even closer accurate recreation to the beta's camera angle.
* Fixed Giganto "Sp02" attack in where there's a charge effect before the beam.
* Fixed Giganto "Sp02" attack in where the laser beam itself is aligned to mouth properly.
* Super Sonic's death scene is updated in Phase 2 to show exposed pillars
* Giganto defeat cutscene is different across Base Story, Battle Rush, and Master King's Trial.

##### **The End (RifleBeast) ShieldTackle**

I compiled together a list of Community Codes in the HMM Code Tab that are currently causing issues with Supreme's Shield Tackle during Phase 2. Although there's a chance it can still occur even without mods, I do not know what causes that. However as far as mods go, I've found several codes that seem to affect the game in this manner. I'll only list the codes that render the fight unplayable.

###### ***If you're using codes or mods from any of the currently listed above, go in to the "Riflebeast Shield Tackle" option in Aggro Titans settings and select: "Disabled" to remove the attack.***



Firstly, any mod that hasn't been updated for Final Horizons will likely cause more issues as a whole due to it being that far outdated.



Secondly, mods that have custom code that adds or changes things to the player. (i.e. Adding a boost aura to Sonic, or certain Physics Tweaks that relate to or have additional tweaks than the codes listed above.)



Thirdly, changes to the "Playercommon.pac" file may have a chance at crashing the game during the Shield Tackle parry, but that's situational and it's hard to pin down exactly where the cause comes from if it happens to be this.



As stated before, this can happen to some people even unmodded and that is the most difficult to pin down. If I missed any codes feel free to let me know in the comments, for now I just wanted to spread more awareness on this.
